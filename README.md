# Equatorial charts (web)

The web version of the Excel workbook `Sen_Test_v2.xlsm`: Swiss Ephemeris RA / De only (no tropical or sidereal
zodiac), with the same rules. Orchestrated by Senthil Muthuraj; built by AI/SI Agents (both shown at the foot of every page's sidebar).

The pages resize with the window as one picture, with all text kept between 10 and 12 px on screen: on a phone or a
narrow window the button rows wrap and each chart is scaled down to the page's width (two fingers enlarge it).

Every page's top line: its name (with its help, ?), the chosen customer's First and Last Name (on AstroClock-2D, AstroClock-3D and Geo/Helio Model the chart's Timer right after it) and, beside it, their DOB (DOB yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm; no time when it is not known) when there is one and the DOB charts are open to them. Then the Now clock, ticking each second: Now yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm:ss UT. On a page with DOB and Now buttons (AstroClock-2D, AstroClock-3D, Geo/Helio Model) only the one picked shows: DOB (the birth) or Now (the clock); neither with Time off (on AstroClock-2D, AstroClock-3D and Geo/Helio Model the chart's own clock follows it: Timer yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm:ss UTC+5.50, the moment the chart shows, started from Now or DOB, whichever is picked, and running on with Rotate in 1s, at the place's UTC offset).

Predictions, Planets Cycles and Planets Distance(%) take a Start Date (UT) and an End Date (UT), dates only (no Time boxes): from the start of the Start Date (a DOB start: the birth date's start) to the end of the End Date.

Every table on every page is just as tall as its rows (no empty rows below them) and keeps its columns close together: each column just wide enough for the longest text it has, or its heading, measured from each character's width in the tables' font and worked out again from the data each time the page draws (so it follows whatever the table holds; no upper limit; the data is not changed; the table only as wide as its columns, scrolling sideways when wider than the page).

The admin tables (App Conditions, Customers, DOB, Page Defaults, Help, Prediction Rules, Symbols), and Logins, list each row's ID first (its number, set by the app and kept across saves; blank on a new row until saved). The admin tables have Insert, Update, Delete and Ignore, each saving at once (no Commit): Insert saves the edits made and adds a blank row to fill in, Update saves the rows as they stand (the new row once filled in), Delete saves the table without the rows ticked, Ignore drops every change not saved yet, Reset to Previous (after asking) puts the table back as it was before its last save (kept in the database at each save; a row deleted since comes back with a new ID; pressed again, it comes back); Aspect Rules has Update, Ignore and Reset to Previous on its title line (back to the orbs before the last Update, never the app's defaults; pressed again, it goes back).

A member whose DOB is not open (Access Now, or End Date passed) who picks DOB is taken to DOB Details, with Get more days of access open, and the message "Your DOB page access expired. Get more days of access in the DOB details page." (End Date passed) or "Get more days of access in the DOB Details page." (never had DOB) on the page (and in the sidebar, when it ended).

Time, on AstroClock-2D, AstroClock-3D and Geo/Helio Model: Now or DOB, one at a time (picking one turns the other off).

Drawing rule: wherever planets are drawn, on every page and graph, a smaller planet sits on top of a bigger one, so that it stays visible when they are close.

Layout rule: everything on all pages stays aligned to the top left.

Font rule: on all pages, text is at most 12 px and at least 10 px.

Now rule: anyone signed in can always see the Now charts, on every chart page: with no birth details, with Access Now or a DOB past its End Date, or with birth details that cannot be worked out (picking DOB then says why it is not open). The Time buttons show DOB first to someone whose Access is DOB, 1Day, DOBT or DOBGT, and Now first to anyone else, DOB still there (a new member picks it to reach the form for their birth details).

Users sign in with Google and see only the customers whose **Email (login)** is theirs. A new member (no customer
has their email yet) is not asked for anything: a Login row goes into the Logins table and they see the Now charts;
picking DOB opens the form for their birth details (also in DOB Details), saved with their login email, and brings
them back to the chart, now with DOB.
A member already there goes straight to their charts. The login email is the key: its own customer is the one with an Access (First Names may repeat), and it may have any
number more with Access blank (people the member added, or a family), which that login sees with DOB access. Admins are listed in the `admins` secret, or have Access Admin.

Access (app/access.py), on the Customers table, one row per login: Now, the Now charts only; DOB, the paid option (until End Date): the DOB and Now charts of every DOB record under their login, people they add (Add a DOB, on DOB Details), and births searched for there without saving (Search Only: a Search choice in the sidebar, which Save this birth there keeps as a person under their login); Admin, an admin. End Date, typed by the admin on Customers (a new DOB with none: 1 day), or extended by any member (Now or DOB, birth details entered or not) with Get more days of access on DOB Details (free for now with the coupon Free, any case; without it nothing is added; to become a paid option) (from End Date while it runs, else from now); a changed Start Date keeps the length; a changed Access counts from now; after End Date, the Now charts only. Someone signing in for the first time gets a row with Now; signing up with their birth details gives DOB for 1 day, which the admin can make longer by typing a later End Date. When DOB (then called 1Day) came to end after 1 day, each such row with no End Date got 1 day from then, once. When the Customers table came in, each login's Access and Access_From were copied into it once from its first DOB record (blank: Now). Earlier Access names are changed to the new ones when the app starts: 1Day, 1Year, DOBG, Individual, Ind, Group and 1wu / 1mu / 1yu to DOB; the 1-day trials DOBT and DOBGT (and Trial-Ind, TrInd, TrDOB, Trial-Grp, TrGrp, TrGroup) to DOB with End Date 1 day after Start Date; Free, FreeInd and FreeGrp to Now.

## Run on this PC

```
cd C:\sweph\web
.venv\Scripts\streamlit run streamlit_app.py --server.address localhost
```

`.streamlit/secrets.toml` (not committed) holds `dev_user`, a test sign-in that works only on localhost.
Customers are kept in `private/app.db` (not committed). On Windows the Swiss Ephemeris DLL
`C:\sweph\bin\swedll64.dll` is used; elsewhere `pyswisseph`.

Check the calculations against the workbook (kept in private/, as it holds a customer's birth data): `.venv\Scripts\python private\tests\check_vs_excel.py`.

## Put it on Streamlit Community Cloud

1. **Database**: create a free Postgres database (Neon or Supabase) and copy its connection URL.
2. **Google sign-in**: in Google Cloud console create an OAuth client ID (Web application) with the redirect URI
   `https://YOUR-APP.streamlit.app/oauth2callback`.
3. **GitHub**: the code is at `smuthuraj-gh/SciAstro` (public, AGPLv3 `LICENSE`). `.venv`, `private` and
   `.streamlit/secrets.toml` are never pushed (`.gitignore` leaves them out).
4. **Deploy**: on share.streamlit.io choose New app, the repository, `streamlit_app.py`; under Advanced settings
   pick Python 3.12 and paste `secrets.example.toml` filled in. Do not add `dev_user`.
5. Sign in with your admin Google account, open **DOB**, import the workbook's Customers CSV, and type each
   customer's Google email in **Email (login)**.
